# Privacy Policy for SwiftSales

**Last Updated:** November 5, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how **SwiftSales** ("the Application", "we", "us", or "our") handles your data. We are committed to protecting your privacy and ensuring that your personal and business information remains secure.

---

## 1. Local Data Storage & Ownership

SwiftSales is an **offline-first** application designed for local management of products, inventory, and sales records.

* **On-Device Storage:** All sales transactions, product details, prices, clerk names, and historical statistics created within SwiftSales are stored **exclusively on your local device** using an encrypted SQLite/Room database.
* **No Remote Servers:** We do not own, operate, or maintain any external servers to store, backup, or process your sales data.
* **Data Ownership:** You retain full ownership and control over all data stored within the Application.

---

## 2. Data Collection & Transmission

* **Zero Personal Data Collection:** SwiftSales does **not** collect, harvest, or transmit any personally identifiable information (PII), device identifiers, IP addresses, or sales logs.
* **No Third-Party Analytics or Trackers:** SwiftSales does not include third-party tracking, telemetric SDKs, or advertising networks (such as Google Analytics, Firebase Analytics, or AdMob).
* **No Network Transmission:** The Application operates entirely offline and does not transmit data over the internet.

---

## 3. Data Export & Sharing Features

SwiftSales allows you to export reports in various formats (such as PDF, CSV, and QuickBooks .iif) or copy text reports to your clipboard:

* **User-Initiated Actions:** Exporting or sharing reports is performed strictly when **you explicitly initiate** the export action.
* **Android FileProvider:** Exported files are shared securely via Android's native `FileProvider` mechanism directly to applications chosen by you (such as your email client, cloud storage app, or messaging app).
* **No Automated Cloud Uploads:** SwiftSales does not automatically upload, send, or sync exported files to any external server.

---

## 4. Permissions Used

SwiftSales requests minimal system capabilities necessary for core functionality:

* **Storage Access / File Sharing:** Used strictly to generate and save temporary export documents (PDF, CSV, QuickBooks) when requested by the user.

---

## 5. Children's Privacy

SwiftSales is a business sales tracking utility and does not address anyone under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

---

## 6. Open Source & Transparency

SwiftSales is open-source software. Users and developers are welcome to inspect the source code to verify our data privacy and security practices.

---

## 7. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect app updates or regulatory changes. Any updates will be posted in this repository with a revised "Last Updated" date.

---
